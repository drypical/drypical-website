Dried Mango

Sun-ripened, rich and naturally candy-sweet.

No added sugar • No preservatives • No artificial colors — just the fruit, dried slowly in Princeton, Texas.

🌍 Origin & history

The mango has been cultivated in India and Burma for more than 4,000 years — long enough to earn the title “king of fruits”. Legend says Buddha rested in a mango grove, and the fruit became a symbol of love and abundance across Asia. Portuguese and Spanish explorers carried it to the Americas in the 1600s and 1700s, and the world has been hooked ever since.

🔎 How we select it

We look for mangoes that are ripe but still firm, heavy with aroma and with smooth, low-fiber flesh. If a fresh mango does not already taste like candy, it does not make it into a Drypical pouch.

🔥 How we dry & pack it

The fruit is peeled and sliced into strips about 5 mm thick, then dried low and slow (around 135 °F) for 8–12 hours. The natural sugars concentrate into a rich, chewy sweetness all on their own — no sugar added, ever. Sealed fresh into 40 g and 200 g pouches.

💪 Goodness inside

Mango is a natural source of vitamins A and C and fiber — the sunshine vitamins, in a pouch. It satisfies a sweet tooth with nothing but the fruit itself.

🍽️ Ways to enjoy it

  • Straight from the pouch, any time of day
  • Rehydrated and blended into smoothies
  • Chopped into salads and homemade salsa
  • In kids’ lunchboxes instead of candy
🥣 With yogurt: fold chopped mango into Greek yogurt, or blend it into a lassi-style smoothie bowl. Mango and yogurt are a classic for a reason.
🍵 Mango infusion: steep a few strips in hot water with chamomile or a slice of ginger. Lovely hot — and superb iced with a squeeze of lime.
