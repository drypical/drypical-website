Our story

Built dollar by dollar. Made to be honest.

My name is José Manuel González, founder of Drypical LLC, and this is the story behind our brand.

For years I worked as a driver in different companies, including an alcoholic beverage factory. While many saw just another production plant, I saw processes, machinery, production lines — and, above all, the possibility of one day creating my own product and my own factory.

During that time, I learned the story of a businesswoman who started in a small barn with the support of her family and a modest investment. Her story deeply inspired me. It showed me that you don't need to start big to go far — you just need to begin with what you have and move forward with discipline, faith, and vision.

I didn't have great financial resources, but I did have determination. I worked for Uber, for delivery services, and in anything necessary to earn money. I bought machines little by little, financing them and paying in installments. Every dollar had a purpose: building something of my own.

At every gas station I visited, I saw the same snacks full of sugar, chemicals, and additives. More than once, tired or in a hurry, I ended up buying products I knew were not good for my health. And that is where the central idea of Drypical was born: to create a real, clean, transparent, and honest snack.

I wanted to develop a product based on something simple yet powerful: fruit. Fruit that is 100% real, carefully selected and dehydrated, with no added sugars, no artificial colors, and no preservatives. A snack that respects your body and your health, while being attractive, practical, and delicious.

That is how Drypical LLC was born — in a small production space I built myself, equipped little by little, always following hygiene, safety, and quality standards, and with a clear vision: to offer products that truly are what they claim to be.

Drypical represents much more than dehydrated fruit. It represents the effort of an entrepreneur who refused to give up, who turned long workdays and difficult moments into motivation to build a brand with purpose — and the belief that it is possible to start a business with limited resources, if there is order, sacrifice, and a strong idea.

Today, Drypical stands for real ingredients, responsible processes, and an authentic experience for our customers. We remain true to these values: transparency, quality, honesty, and respect for the health of those who enjoy our products.

This is only the beginning. Drypical is taking its first steps, but it was created to grow, to take root, and to go far — always maintaining the essence that gave it life: being a real brand, with real fruit and real flavor.

Questions, answered straight

Before you ask — here it is.

Is there really no added sugar?

Correct. The sweetness comes from the fruit itself. We add no sugar, no syrups, no artificial colors and no preservatives.

What is the ingredient list?

For our single-fruit pouches, the list is just that fruit — pineapple is only pineapple. Our Tropical Mix is a blend of real fruits only. In every pouch: no added sugar, no oil, no salt and nothing artificial.
